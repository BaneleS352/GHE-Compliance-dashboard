Employees will log into the application through the Oracle integration, using their employee code and password. The employee code will be passed as a parameter to the HIS SQL integration.		
Once authenticated, the employee code will be used to retrieve the employee’s latest available information from HIS. This information should automatically populate the relevant employee fields within Section 1 of the form		
The HIS employee information should identify and link each Team Member to their respective manager. This will ensure that the correct Approving Manager Name is populated and that the manager’s email address can be used for workflow notifications.		
Tyra will work with Santh and Shareel to establish the SQL integration process and incorporate the HIS employee information into the application.
